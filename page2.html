ABOUT THE TOPIC

What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a branch of computer science that focuses on creating machines and systems capable of performing tasks that normally require human intelligence.

AI systems can learn from data, recognize patterns, solve problems, understand language, and make decisions.

Applications of AI

Robotics

AI helps robots learn and perform different tasks.

Healthcare

AI helps doctors analyze data and detect diseases.

Education

AI provides smart learning tools for students.
